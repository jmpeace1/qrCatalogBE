Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · SuperCalc for Android

In short

  • Your calculations, labels and settings stay on your phone. SuperCalc has no account and no server of its own.
  • The camera is used only while you read a number with it. Pictures are read on the phone and never saved or sent.
  • Google ML Kit, which reads the numbers, sends Google basic diagnostics (device model, app version, performance and error codes). It never sends your pictures or what they say.
  • No ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

What SuperCalc keeps on your phone

Everything below is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent anywhere by SuperCalc.

DataWhat it is
HistoryYour calculations and results, with the time of each, and any pins, labels and line numbers you add.
Backup slotsUp to three copies of your history that you save from the History menu.
SettingsYour choices, such as decimals, colors, which pads show and where you placed them.
Receipt imagesMade only when you choose Export, kept in temporary storage for sharing, and cleared by Android over time.

You can delete your history from the History menu at any time. Uninstalling SuperCalc removes all of this data from your phone.

Camera

SuperCalc asks for the camera only when you tap the camera on the advanced pad. While it is on, the picture shows in the input area and Google ML Kit's text recognition, which runs on your phone, looks for a number in it. The number found is typed into the calculator. Pictures are not stored, and neither they nor the text read from them leave your phone.

You can turn off camera access at any time in Android's settings. Everything else in SuperCalc works without it.

Diagnostics sent by Google ML Kit

Reading numbers uses Google ML Kit. According to Google's ML Kit data disclosure, ML Kit sends Google the following for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypted over HTTPS, and does not pass it to third parties:

DataExamples
Device informationManufacturer, model, Android version and build, hardware accelerators
App informationPackage name and app version
IdentifierA per-installation identifier, not meant to identify you or your device
PerformanceHow long reading took, image format and size, feature version, error codes

This data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. SuperCalc itself does not receive it.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include SuperCalc's data (history, backup slots and settings) in your device backup and restore it on a new phone. This is done by Android and Google under your backup settings, which you can change in Android's settings.

Sharing

SuperCalc shares something only when you ask it to: a receipt image you export, or a number you copy. Where it goes is up to you and the app you pick in Android's share menu.

What SuperCalc does not do

  • It shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking of its own.
  • It does not sell, rent or trade any data.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, files or microphone.

Children

SuperCalc is a general calculator and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. A change that affects what data is handled will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
